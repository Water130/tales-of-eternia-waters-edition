Tales of Eternia - Water's Edition - 0.10.4 test release

An experimental combat and UI mod for Tales of Eternia Remastered on Steam.

Created, designed and directed by Water, a human. All of the mod's code was
written by AI. This mod has bugs, and unknown bugs or new regressions may appear
in current and future versions. Not all bugs will necessarily be fixed.
Fixes, support and future updates are not guaranteed.

Use at your own risk. Provided as-is, without warranty. To the extent permitted
by applicable law, the creator accepts no liability for problems or losses
resulting from its use, including damage to game files, save corruption or lost
progress. Back up your saves separately before installing. The installer does
not directly edit saves, but gameplay bugs may still affect your progress.

Current features include TP recovery from basic physical hits, adjusted
post-battle TP recovery, a smoother player-and-target camera, reduced spell
freezes, and limited overlapping spell effects. You can choose a full-vitality
Craymel summon by holding the Arte command through an elemental cast, while L1
holds a completed spell instead.

The interface adds animated HP/TP gauges, casting and item cooldown bars,
element-colored casting effects and damage numbers, plus animated Craymel
miniatures with colored vitality counters. Startup goes straight to the opening.

The title screen features a blue Water's Edition wordmark with a cyan glow,
visible after the original title reveal and before pressing Start.
Version 0.10.4 fixes the title logo drawing over the settings page. The page
follows the native Options layout, with gold labels, side-by-side choices,
a hand cursor, Reset/Default controls and live keyboard/controller button hints.
The Mod settings page on the title screen lets you toggle the camera, both TP
recovery features, damage colors, custom HUD, casting bars and item cooldown bar
independently. Settings are saved automatically. Mod text is French with the
French game text language, and English with every other language.

This is a TEST release. The 0.10.4 settings menu has been confirmed in-game by
the tester. Some camera edges, summon backdrops and the newest Spread cap
still need broader visual feedback.
Same-effect and special spells are still queued.
A Compatibility mode restores the native spell queue while keeping the other
changes.

Requires Windows and Steam build 25190541. Extract the ZIP, close the game,
double-click Install.cmd, then type Y and press Enter. The game folder is detected
automatically. To remove the mod, run Uninstall.cmd and confirm the same way.
The installer checks the exact game version and backs up the original DLL.
The ZIP contains patch data, not the complete game DLL.

If uninstalling fails, close the game and try Steam's game-file verification:
https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/0C48-FCBD-DA71-93EB
Reinstalling the game may be necessary if problems persist. Verification and
reinstallation do not guarantee recovery of damaged saves or lost progress.
