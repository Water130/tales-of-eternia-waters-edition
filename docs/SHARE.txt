Water's Edition is an experimental overhaul for Tales of Eternia Remastered on Windows / Steam.
Version 0.14.11 adds three Artesphere pages, five spell growth tracks, 72 Craymel
faculties, earned spirit EXP, Farah Energy and standalone team-up artes.
Most features can be toggled, with related progression systems grouped together.
Mod text is French or English.

Download, installation and features:
https://github.com/Water130/tales-of-eternia-waters-edition
https://www.nexusmods.com/talesofeterniaremastered/mods/13

Created and directed by Water. All mod code was written by AI.

guaranteed. Use at your own risk and back up your saves and mod preferences.
